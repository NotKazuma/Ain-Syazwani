An apology

I'm Sorry

This part is harder to write than the rest. Not every page can be about the good moments — some have to be about the times I let you down.

So here it is, plainly, without dressing it up: I'm sorry. These are the words I owe you.

I

Where I Fell Short

There were days you needed more than I knew how to give,A calmer kind of patience, a gentler way to live.I answered you too slowly, I noticed things too late,And let the little silences grow heavy with their weight.

I told myself you'd understand, that there was always time,That love could fill the empty space between each careless line.But love is in the showing up, not only in the word,And I'm sorry for the moments when you felt unheard.

You never asked for perfect, you only asked for true,For someone who would notice all the quiet things you do.And if I missed them, even once, I need you to know:It wasn't that I didn't care — I just learned too slow.

II

Not Enough

Some nights I lie awake and count the ways I've fallen short,The moments I was distant when you needed my support.You deserve someone steady, someone certain, someone whole,And I'm still only learning how to give you all my soul.

I'm not the finished version of the man I want to be,I'm rough around the edges, still unsure of half of me.But every flaw I carry, I can see it clearer now,And seeing is the first step — I'm still learning how.

So if I wasn't enough for you on days you needed more,I'm sorry that you had to knock on such a heavy door.I'm not asking you to settle, or to wait on me forever,Just let me show you I can grow — and grow with you, together.

III

What I Can Promise

I can't promise I won't stumble, I can't promise I won't fall,I can't promise every answer or the perfect words at all.But I can promise honesty, and effort every day,And when I'm wrong, I'll own it, not just hope it goes away.

I'll listen when you're quiet, I'll ask instead of guess,I'll show up on the ordinary days, not just the best.I'll learn the way you need to be loved, not the way I planned,And hold on to you gently, with a softer, steadier hand.

You were never too much, Ain — I just wasn't yet enough,But I'm staying, and I'm growing, even when it's rough.So here's my heart, a little bruised, but honest, and still true:I'm sorry. And I'm trying. And I'm still choosing you.

If you're still reading this far, thank you for giving me the chance to say it.
