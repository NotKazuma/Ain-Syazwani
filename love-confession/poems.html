Our First Chapter

I haven't discovered all of you yet,and perhaps that's what makes this beautiful.

You're not from my yesterday,not someone I'm trying to fix —you're something new,someone I want to understandfrom the very first page.

I won't claim to be flawless,or that I'll get everything right.

But I want to try —to learn you,to care for you,to build something genuinewithout rushing what we're becoming.

I'm not here out of routine.I'm here because I picked you.

And in this opening chapter,still being written…

I'll do my bestto bring you joy,one step at a time,with nothing from the past weighing us down.
