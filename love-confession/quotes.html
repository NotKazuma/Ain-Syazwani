Words from My Heart

These lines capture what I feel for you – honest thoughts from deep within.

  • "You mean the world to me, beyond what words can describe."
  • "My heart made its choice. I offer you my sincere love."
  • "You bring light to my every day. I belong to you."
  • "When I'm with you, I've found my comfort. My love for you has no end."
  • "Time spent with you is precious. You carry my heart."
  • "I admit it: without you, something feels missing."
  • "You're everything I ever wanted. My love stretches to infinity."
  • "My soul found its match. This is my vow to you."
  • "Each day I fall for you more. You are my joy."
  • "Our story begins here. I love you, now and always."
