I

The Sudden Shift

It’s strange how quickly a story line can bend,How a simple contact name stops being “just a friend.”Yesterday you were just another thought passing through my head,Now your morning message is the very first thing I read.

There were no dramatic movie scenes, no light up in the sky,Just a sudden realization of how fast the hours flyWhenever you’re around, or when your name pops on my screen,Flipping every single ordinary moment in between.

I used to move through daily routines without a second thought,Now I'm tangled up in every little moment that you caught.It’s a brand new baseline, a total shift in how I view my day,And honestly, I wouldn't have it any other way.

II

Early Days & Car Park Conversations

We’re still figuring out the exact mechanics of how this fits,Navigating the quiet moments and the awkward little bits.Learning which of my dumb jokes land and which ones hit the wall,Trying to figure out the right time for a late-night call.

Sitting parked in the car for hours after driving you back home,Talking about absolute nonsense, neither reaching for a phone.The engine's turned off, the dashboard lights are fading low,And neither one of us is in a hurry to get up and go.

It’s unwritten, it’s brand new, it’s slightly clumsy at the start,But watching it unfold is the most exciting part.We’re building a foundation out of simple, quiet nights,Just two people getting it right beneath the streetlights.

III

Overthinking the Unsent Text

I catch myself overthinking every single thing I send,Typing out a paragraph, erasing it, changing the end.Trying so hard to play it cool and keep a steady pace,While secretly just counting down the hours till I see your face.

Do I send a double text or wait for you to reply?Why am I acting like a amateur, over-analyzing why?It’s hilarious, it’s exhausting, but it’s real as it can be—This ridiculous, amazing phase of letting someone in to see.

I drop the cool facade the second your message lights up bright,Realizing all that overthinking wasn't worth the fight.It's just us figuring it out, one draft at a time,Finding the balance in this brand new rhythm and rhyme.

IV

The Notification High

My phone lies on the desk, face down while I try to work,Every minor vibration turns me into a watchful jerk.I glance over immediately, hoping it’s your name appearing,Cutting through the busy noise and everything I’m hearing.

It’s crazy how a simple three-word line sent on the flyCan completely change my mood and raise my spirits high.A random photo of your lunch, a quick meme out of nowhere,Just a subtle little reminder that you’re sitting over there.

It brightens up the heavy hours, turns the grey into clear blue,Proving how easily my whole day turns around because of you.Not over-the-top, just a steady, subtle little spark,A tiny little lantern cutting straight through the dark.

V

Small Talks & Unfiltered Hours

We spend hours talking about nothing—our days, our food,The silly little random things that put us in a mood.We don’t need deep philosophical debates or poetic prose,It’s just easy, unscripted, and that’s how the evening goes.

I don’t have to keep up a performance or put on a show,I can just sit back, relax, and let the real version show.Messy hair, tired eyes, and stories from work that drag on long,Yet with you listening on the line, nothing feels out of song.

We’re stripping away the polished surface people usually display,Finding comfort in the casual things we choose to say.Building something genuine out of everyday background noise,Finding our natural cadence, finding our shared voice.

VI

The Butterfly Phase

That sudden, nervous flutter right before we’re set to meet,Walking side by side down an ordinary, crowded street.My hands are in my pockets, wondering if I should reach out,Hoping I’m making sense, hoping I don’t give you reason to doubt.

Every time our shoulders bump, my brain goes momentarily blank,Forgetting what I was saying, losing track of my own bankOf thoughts and clever lines I spent all afternoon preparing,Replaced by the sheer distraction of the glance that you’re sharing.

It’s the butterfly phase—clumsy, raw, and slightly out of control,The kind of good nervousness that takes over your entire soul.A little bit chaotic, but the sweetest kind of rush,Hiding a ridiculous grin behind a sudden, simple blush.

VII

Inside Jokes & Coded Language

We’ve already started building our own private little language,A collection of weird references that only we can manage.A quick side-eye across a room full of people we know,Communicating entire sentences without making a show.

An old joke from three days ago that makes no sense to anyone else,Stored away like little treasures kept high up on the shelf.It’s early in the game, but the vault is filling fastWith memories and funny moments made to actually last.

When standard words fall short, an inside joke steps in,Rewinding back to that moment with a subtle, knowing grin.It’s our own tiny world tucked inside the bigger one outside,Where every little glance becomes a secret place to hide.

VIII

My Favorite Distraction

I’m supposed to be focused, I’ve got a long task list today,But my mind keeps taking detours and wandering your way.I stare at a blank screen, replaying how you laughed last night,Remembering the way your hair looked in the dim restaurant light.

Work can wait a few extra minutes while I relive the conversation,Lost entirely in this brand new, wonderful distraction.You’ve officially taken over the background of my mind,Pushing all the stressful, boring worries far behind.

I try to pull myself together and act like I'm locked in,Then your text comes through and the distraction starts again.And honestly? I wouldn't trade this lack of focus for the world,Watching this new dynamic slowly get unfurled.

IX

Sanctuary in the Noise

The rest of the world moves fast, demanding way too much,Constantly rushing around, out of tune and out of touch.But stepping into our space feels like hitting a total pause,Escaping all the daily stress, the expectations, and the laws.

With you, there’s no pressure to be impressive or act grand,Just sitting side by side, enjoying where we land.A safe haven built without a single brick or wooden beam,Much more grounded and reliable than any distant dream.

When the day gets heavy and the noise begins to rise,I find an instant sense of quiet looking in your eyes.It’s a brand new sanctuary, simple, quiet, and clear,Where all the outside clutter just seems to disappear.

X

Taking It Day by Day

We don't need to overthink where this goes down the line,Or try to map out future years like some master design.We don’t have to rush the steps or stress about what’s next,Analyzing every milestone like a complicated text.

Let’s just take it day by day, enjoying where we are right now,Appreciating how we got here without worrying about howThe future holds its pieces, or what tomorrow has to bring,As long as we keep enjoying the simple, present thing.

Here’s to the nervous beginnings, the awkward moments and the grace,Taking every single step together at our own steady pace.Just two people starting out, hands intertwined and free,Excited for the next chapter of whatever this will be.
