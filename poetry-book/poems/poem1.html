1. "The Sudden Shift"

It’s strange how quickly a story line can bend,
How a simple contact name stops being “just a friend.”
Yesterday you were just another thought passing through my head,
Now your morning message is the very first thing I read.

There were no dramatic movie scenes, no light up in the sky,
Just a sudden realization of how fast the hours fly
Whenever you’re around, or when your name pops on my screen,
Flipping every single ordinary moment in between.

I used to move through daily routines without a second thought,
Now I'm tangled up in every little moment that you caught.
It’s a brand new baseline, a total shift in how I view my day,
And honestly, I wouldn't have it any other way.
