10. "Taking It Day by Day"

We don't need to overthink where this goes down the line,
Or try to map out future years like some master design.
We don’t have to rush the steps or stress about what’s next,
Analyzing every milestone like a complicated text.

Let’s just take it day by day, enjoying where we are right now,
Appreciating how we got here without worrying about how
The future holds its pieces, or what tomorrow has to bring,
As long as we keep enjoying the simple, present thing.

Here’s to the nervous beginnings, the awkward moments and the grace,
Taking every single step together at our own steady pace.
Just two people starting out, hands intertwined and free,
Excited for the next chapter of whatever this will be.
