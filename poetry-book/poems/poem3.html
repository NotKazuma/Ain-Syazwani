3. "Overthinking the Unsent Text"

I catch myself overthinking every single thing I send,
Typing out a paragraph, erasing it, changing the end.
Trying so hard to play it cool and keep a steady pace,
While secretly just counting down the hours till I see your face.

Do I send a double text or wait for you to reply?
Why am I acting like a amateur, over-analyzing why?
It’s hilarious, it’s exhausting, but it’s real as it can be—
This ridiculous, amazing phase of letting someone in to see.

I drop the cool facade the second your message lights up bright,
Realizing all that overthinking wasn't worth the fight.
It's just us figuring it out, one draft at a time,
Finding the balance in this brand new rhythm and rhyme.
