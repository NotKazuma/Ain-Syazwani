4. "The Notification High"

My phone lies on the desk, face down while I try to work,
Every minor vibration turns me into a watchful jerk.
I glance over immediately, hoping it’s your name appearing,
Cutting through the busy noise and everything I’m hearing.

It’s crazy how a simple three-word line sent on the fly
Can completely change my mood and raise my spirits high.
A random photo of your lunch, a quick meme out of nowhere,
Just a subtle little reminder that you’re sitting over there.

It brightens up the heavy hours, turns the grey into clear blue,
Proving how easily my whole day turns around because of you.
Not over-the-top, just a steady, subtle little spark,
A tiny little lantern cutting straight through the dark.
