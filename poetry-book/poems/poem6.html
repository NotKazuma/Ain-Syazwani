6. "The Butterfly Phase"

That sudden, nervous flutter right before we’re set to meet,
Walking side by side down an ordinary, crowded street.
My hands are in my pockets, wondering if I should reach out,
Hoping I’m making sense, hoping I don’t give you reason to doubt.

Every time our shoulders bump, my brain goes momentarily blank,
Forgetting what I was saying, losing track of my own bank
Of thoughts and clever lines I spent all afternoon preparing,
Replaced by the sheer distraction of the glance that you’re sharing.

It’s the butterfly phase—clumsy, raw, and slightly out of control,
The kind of good nervousness that takes over your entire soul.
A little bit chaotic, but the sweetest kind of rush,
Hiding a ridiculous grin behind a sudden, simple blush.
