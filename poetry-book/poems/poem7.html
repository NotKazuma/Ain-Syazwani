7. "Inside Jokes & Coded Language"

We’ve already started building our own private little language,
A collection of weird references that only we can manage.
A quick side-eye across a room full of people we know,
Communicating entire sentences without making a show.

An old joke from three days ago that makes no sense to anyone else,
Stored away like little treasures kept high up on the shelf.
It’s early in the game, but the vault is filling fast
With memories and funny moments made to actually last.

When standard words fall short, an inside joke steps in,
Rewinding back to that moment with a subtle, knowing grin.
It’s our own tiny world tucked inside the bigger one outside,
Where every little glance becomes a secret place to hide.
