8. "My Favorite Distraction"

I’m supposed to be focused, I’ve got a long task list today,
But my mind keeps taking detours and wandering your way.
I stare at a blank screen, replaying how you laughed last night,
Remembering the way your hair looked in the dim restaurant light.

Work can wait a few extra minutes while I relive the conversation,
Lost entirely in this brand new, wonderful distraction.
You’ve officially taken over the background of my mind,
Pushing all the stressful, boring worries far behind.

I try to pull myself together and act like I'm locked in,
Then your text comes through and the distraction starts again.
And honestly? I wouldn't trade this lack of focus for the world,
Watching this new dynamic slowly get unfurled.
