IX

Sanctuary in the Noise

The rest of the world moves fast, demanding way too much,Constantly rushing around, out of tune and out of touch.But stepping into our space feels like hitting a total pause,Escaping all the daily stress, the expectations, and the laws.

With you, there’s no pressure to be impressive or act grand,Just sitting side by side, enjoying where we land.A safe haven built without a single brick or wooden beam,Much more grounded and reliable than any distant dream.

When the day gets heavy and the noise begins to rise,I find an instant sense of quiet looking in your eyes.It’s a brand new sanctuary, simple, quiet, and clear,Where all the outside clutter just seems to disappear.
